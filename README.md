## Full quality, not re-encodes

Patreon serves creator video as an adaptive stream. Fanripper takes the **top rendition** and writes a clean MP4 with sound — never re-encoded — so what you keep is the best quality your membership can play. Patreon video isn't Widevine-locked, so there's no decryption step and nothing to go wrong.

Photo sets and audio posts save as the **original files** Patreon stores, named by what they actually are.

Nothing is uploaded and your Patreon password is never asked for. It works through the session you're already signed into.

## What it saves

| | |
|---|---|
| **Posts** | Creator videos, photo sets and audio, with a save button on each |
| **Group chats** | The media in the group chats you're a member of |
| **A whole creator** | "Back up this creator" pages through their entire feed for you |
| **Bulk** | Paced, resumable, already-saved files skipped |

Point Fanripper at a creator you support and it walks their whole page — paced like ordinary browsing, resuming where it stopped and skipping files you already have.

## Honest limits

- **Members-only posts you haven't unlocked are left alone.** Only content your membership can already open is saved; no paywall is bypassed.
- The per-chat capture reads the media your account can see.
- Comments aren't saved.

## On your phone (iPhone & Android)

Patreon works in the Fanripper phone app too — same single saves, whole-creator backups and group-chat media, on iOS and Android.

1. Install **Expo Go** (free) from the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/expo-go/id982107779) or [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=host.exp.exponent).
2. Open **[fanripper.com/app](https://fanripper.com/app)** on your phone and tap **Open Fanripper in Expo Go**, or scan the code there with your phone's camera.
3. Sign in with your Fanripper email: same account and plan as the extension.

Your phone gets its own device slot, so it never signs out your computer. Downloads run while the app is open, and updates arrive on their own.

## Install the extension (about 60 seconds)

1. Download `fanripper.zip` from [install.fanripper.com](https://install.fanripper.com) or the [main repo's Releases](https://github.com/SatyamHitman/fanripper/releases).
2. Unzip it somewhere permanent.
3. Open `chrome://extensions` and turn on **Developer mode**.
4. Click **Load unpacked** and pick the unzipped folder.
5. Open Patreon. A save button appears on content you can already see.

Works on Chrome, Brave and Edge. It is not in the Chrome Web Store because stores remove extensions that work with subscription content.

**Updating:** a load-unpacked install cannot update itself. Fanripper tells you when a new version ships; extract it over the same folder and hit **Reload**. About 15 seconds, and your settings and login are kept.
